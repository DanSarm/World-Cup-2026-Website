World Cup logo — drop your image here
=====================================

Save your logo as:

  world-cup-logo.png

Supported formats: PNG (recommended), SVG, WebP, or JPG.
If you use a different filename or format, update SITE_LOGO_PATH in lib/site.ts.

Tips:
  - Square or wide horizontal works best
  - At least 256×256 px (512+ for retina)
  - Transparent background looks best on the dark site theme

After saving the file, refresh the browser — no rebuild needed.
